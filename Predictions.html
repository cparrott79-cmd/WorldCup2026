📖 Scoring Format

Each game is worth up to 8 points, earned in four independent ways:

What you get rightPoints
✅ Correct outcome (right winner, or a draw)+2
✅ Exact home-team score+2
✅ Exact away-team score+2
🎯 Spot-on bonus (all three above correct)+2
Maximum per game8
🏆 The Final counts double — every point is ×2 (up to 16)

Knockout games: if you predict a draw, your “who goes through” pick counts as the outcome — get it right and that’s your +2 for the correct outcome.
